# Admissions (basics)

AskMak’s curated help for admissions stays **high level** unless the **knowledge base** or **tools** surface **official wording**.

**Check admission status:** Follow the **current application or admissions portal** linked from **www.mak.ac.ug** for your application cycle. Keep your **application ID** or **reference** handy.

**Documents to report:** Lists vary by **programme** and **year**—only repeat **specific document names** if they appear in the **knowledge base** or an **official page** you retrieved. Otherwise direct users to the **latest admissions brochure or checklist** on the university site.

**How to apply:** New applicants should use the **official online application** and deadlines published for that intake; do **not invent** deadlines or cut-offs in chat.

**When to use a support ticket in this app:** For **portal login or technical problems** during application (cannot upload, payment page errors, account issues), use **“Can’t find your answer? Submit a support ticket”** so ICT or admissions support can assist via the **admin workflow**. Purely **policy** questions may still need the **admissions office** if not in the KB.
