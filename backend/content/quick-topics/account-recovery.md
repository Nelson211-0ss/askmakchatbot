# Account recovery

**Account recovery** is for **lost access**, **suspected compromise**, or **outdated recovery details**.

**Hacked or suspicious activity:** Change your password through **official reset** as soon as you can, sign out other sessions if the service offers it, and **alert staff** through **“Can’t find your answer? Submit a support ticket”** so logs and permissions can be reviewed.

**Lost access to recovery email:** If you **cannot receive** reset messages, you will usually need **identity verification with staff**—use the **in-app ticket** and prepare **student ID / registration** information as instructed after submission.

**Changed phone number:** Update **recovery** numbers in **each** system that stores them (webmail, portal profile, etc.). If the portal **blocks updates** without old verification, **submit a support ticket** for a manual update.

**Consistency:** Prefer **one set of strong credentials** for university SSO where systems share identity; avoid reusing **personal** passwords.

**When to use a support ticket in this app:** Any case where **self-service cannot finish** or **security is in doubt**—the **Submit a support ticket** path connects you to **administrators** who respond through the **app’s ticketing workflow and email**.
