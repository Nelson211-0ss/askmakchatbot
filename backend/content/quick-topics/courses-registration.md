# Courses and registration

This topic aligns with **course registration**, **add/drop**, and **viewing registered courses** in **university systems**.

**Register for the semester:** Use **ACMIS** or the **system your college mandates** during the **published registration window**. If a course is **full** or **restricted**, the portal will usually show a reason; **overrides** need **department or college** approval, not the chatbot.

**Add or drop:** Follow **portal menus** for **add/drop** within **allowed dates**; late changes often need **academic office** approval.

**See registered courses:** The **student portal** typically lists **current registration** under academics or registration tabs.

**When to use a support ticket in this app:** If the **portal errors** on every attempt, **courses disappear** after successful save, or **technical blocks** stop registration near deadlines—open **[Submit a support ticket](#support-ticket)** so staff can fix records or permissions and **email** you when resolved.
