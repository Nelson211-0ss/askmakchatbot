# ACMIS / Student portal

This topic covers **ACMIS** and related **student portal** access at Makerere.

**Logging in:** Use your official **student credentials** on the **ACMIS / student portal** URL provided by the university (check **www.mak.ac.ug** or **MyPortal** for the current link). Use a current browser; avoid saving passwords on shared computers.

**Forgot password:** Use the portal’s **Forgot password / Reset** option if shown. Complete **identity verification** (often university email or student number) exactly as prompted—**do not share your password** with anyone, including chat support.

**Course registration:** Registration windows and menus are controlled by the **academic calendar** and your programme rules. If the portal opens but registration actions fail or courses are missing while peers can register, treat it as a **system or records issue**.

**Exam results:** Results usually appear under the results or academics section when the examination office releases them. If others in your programme see grades and you do not (after a reasonable wait), your record may need a **staff check**.

**When to use a support ticket in this app:** After you follow official on-screen steps, if you still **cannot log in**, **reset fails**, **registration errors persist**, or **results never appear** while others have access—open **[Submit a support ticket](#support-ticket)** so **administrators can respond** (including by **email** on resolution). This in-chat ticket flow is the primary support path for account and portal problems surfaced here.
