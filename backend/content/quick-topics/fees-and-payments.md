# Fees and payments on the portal

This topic covers **tuition and fees** as they appear on **university systems** (balances, PRN, and payment reflection).

**Check balance and PRN:** Log in to the **official student fees / finance area** via **ACMIS**, **MyPortal**, or the path your college publishes. **Payment Reference Numbers (PRN)** and balances are shown in those systems when configured for your cohort—**exact labels change over time**, so follow what the live portal displays.

**Paid but not reflecting:** Keep your **bank receipt** and **PRN**. Allow **at least one business day** for some payment channels to post. If the amount still does not show after the usual delay and your **bank confirms** the debit, you may need **finance or registry** to reconcile the payment—this often requires **staff action** with your proof of payment.

**Deadlines:** **Tuition deadlines** vary by semester and notice from the university. AskMak only states **specific dates** if they appear in the **knowledge base** or a **fetched official page**; otherwise say you do not have the current calendar and point to **www.mak.ac.ug** or your **college notice board**.

**When to use a support ticket in this app:** If the portal shows **wrong balance after correct payment**, **PRN errors**, or **blocks** that stop registration despite payment—use **[Submit a support ticket](#support-ticket)** so staff can trace the transaction and email you when it is fixed.
